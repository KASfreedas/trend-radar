Settings — Hashtag Growth
Track Any Hashtag's Growth

Every hashtag on your Instagram/TikTok watch lists already gets its posting volume recorded on each real refresh — this is that full history, not just the top 3 shown on the dashboard. Add a hashtag to a watch list above, let it survive 2+ real refreshes, and its growth (or decline) shows up here.

Only posts/videos within this recency window AND clearing every minimum below show up as a "top post." These are deliberately high bars — an empty result means nothing recent cleared them, which is a real answer, not a bug. Loosen these if that happens more than you'd like.

Settings — Flavor Chart
Which Lines Show on the Dashboard Chart

By default the chart auto-shows whichever 5 flavor keywords are trending highest right now. Pick up to 5 specific ones instead if you'd rather always see the same lines — leave nothing checked to go back to auto.

Settings — Discovery Rules
What Shows Up as a Suggestion

These rules decide which hashtags and keywords surface as new-flavor suggestions — the same idea as the Top Post Thresholds, but for the discovery feed. Tune them when you see too much noise or too few ideas.

Settings — Competitors
Tracked Competitors

Real competitor Instagram accounts. Each scan checks whether any of them just posted something overperforming their own normal engagement — a real post, with a link, the briefing can cite. Scanning costs Apify credits per account and only runs when you click Scan.

Settings — Past Picks
Did They Sell?

Every “Launch Next” and “Menu Gap” item gets logged here automatically when a briefing generates. Mark what actually happened — once a few resolve, future briefings open with a real hit-rate.

Settings — Market Clusters
Your Locations & Markets

Group your locations into markets that behave alike. Each cluster has its own neighborhood hashtags (drive the per-market local buzz) and local competitors. Menu and Brand DNA stay shared — it's one brand. Geo-hashtag edits apply on the next refresh.

How To Read This
Most trustworthy — Sales Tracker suggestions, once logged: your own real numbers, not a scraped guess.
Solid — High-confidence picks: Google search data backed by 3+ independent sources.
Concrete but narrow — Competitor Watch: a specific real post that really happened, worth a look either way.
Worth watching, not betting on — Medium/low-confidence gaps and Suggested Keywords: early signal, not proof yet.
